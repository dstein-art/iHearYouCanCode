SuperDirt Sample Library

Browse, preview, and copy URLs from the classic Dirt-Samples collection — the same sample folders used by TidalCycles live coding.

SuperDirt is the SuperCollider audio engine behind TidalCycles. It loads short audio files from named folders (like bd, hh, or 808bd) and triggers them on demand over Open Sound Control. The samples listed here are hosted for direct browser access — preview them inline or copy a URL into your own project.

Credit · SuperDirt project

Sample folders originate from the Dirt-Samples collection bundled with the SuperDirt ecosystem. SuperDirt is free software maintained by Julian Rohrhuber, Alex McLean, and contributors.

The audio files listed here are hosted on David Stein's Amazon S3 bucket for preview and experimentation. If you are using these samples in a distributed or public-facing project, please upload the sound files to your own server rather than linking directly to this hosting.
